Asked for chill, vibey house, he rejected five things in a row from one
record, and **not one of them was rejected for its sound design**:

| What | What he said |
|---|---|
| A modal vibraphone | "эти звуки очень высокие... как будто им здесь не место" |
| An alto saxophone | "какой-то вокал... это совсем выбивается из общего настроения" |
| An FM Rhodes chord | "оно всё равно очень резкое... должно быть мягкое" |
| Four 909 open hats a bar | "как будто вот этот песочный шум всегда... перегружает" |
| The chord at −11 dB | "чересчур... долбит по ушам... заполняет весь диапазон" |

Every one of those was measured, physically modelled and correct. They were
still wrong, because in this genre the question is not *is this a good sound*
but *does this need to be audible at all*.

Two sentences of his are the whole brief and both are literal:

- **"надо что-то типа чил, релакс, минимал-хаус"** - the ceiling on how many
  things may be heard at once is about six, and a lead instrument is not one
  of them. The chord progression is the tune.
- **"мне нравилось, когда оно было внизу, в фоне, в бэкграунде, очень-очень
  далеко"** - this is not a mood note. It is three numbers: voiced low (MIDI
  48-67, under the register any lead would use), mixed 8-11 dB under the
  floor, and sent to a reverb that is more wet than dry.

**Why:** the instinct when a mix measures thin in a band is to add a voice
that fills it, and in this genre that instinct is exactly backwards - the
hole is the genre. He identifies a new element as an intrusion within one
listen, every time, and no amount of tasteful writing rescues a part that
should not exist.

**How to apply:** for anything he calls chill, relaxed or minimal, build the
record from a floor, a bass and ONE harmonic voice, and spend the development
on filter, density and register rather than on new instruments. Before adding
a voice, ask what it is in front of. See [[section-contrast-belongs-in-level]],
[[an-open-hat-must-end-before-the-next-one]] and, for the same instinct caught
one instrument at a time, [[bells-are-not-a-default-top-layer]].
