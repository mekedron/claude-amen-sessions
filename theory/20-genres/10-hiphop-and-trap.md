# Hip-Hop and Trap

**Identity:** the beat as a bed for the voice. Everything is arranged around
leaving space for rapping — the least crowded mid-range in popular music.

## Boom bap

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Tempo | 85–95 BPM |
| Meter | 4/4 |
| Swing | 54–62% |
| Key | Minor; often just whatever key the sample is in |
| Loudness | −10 to −8 LUFS |

### Drums
```
kick:   x - - - | - - x - | - - x - | - - - -
snare:  - - - - | x - - - | - - - - | x - - -
hat:    x - x . | x - x . | x - x . | x - x .
```
- Sampled, dusty, filtered. Often the drums come from the same record as the
  melody.
- Snare late by 10–20 ms; hats swung.
- Ghost notes and open hats for the "loose" feel.
- Vinyl noise underneath everything.

### Sampling
The core craft:
1. Find a 1–4 bar loop from a soul, jazz or funk record.
2. Chop it (by transient or by 16th) and re-sequence.
3. Filter (often low-pass at 3–8 kHz, or high-pass to isolate a horn stab).
4. Pitch it up or down; time-stretch to tempo.
5. Layer your own drums on top, louder than the sample.
6. Add a sub bass following the sample's root notes.

Techniques: **chipmunk soul** (pitch a vocal sample up), **filtered loop**
(low-pass so only the bass and body remain), **stab chopping** (one chord from
the record, played as an instrument).

## Trap

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Tempo | 130–160 written, **felt at 65–80** (halftime) |
| Meter | 4/4 |
| Key | Minor, Phrygian, harmonic minor |
| Loudness | −9 to −7 LUFS |

### Drums
```
kick:   x - - - | - - x - | - - - x | - - - -
snare:  - - - - | - - - - | x - - - | - - - -
hat:    x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x r r r
open:   - - - - | - - x - | - - - - | - - - -
```
- **Snare/clap on beat 3 only** (halftime).
- **Hi-hat rolls** are the identity: 1/8 → 1/16 → 1/32 → triplets, changing
  every bar or half-bar. Pitch and pan them for movement.
- Kick pattern is syncopated and follows the 808.
- 808 open hat on the "and" of 2 or 4.
- Rim, snap, and pitched percussion fill the space.

### The 808
- A tuned sine with a long decay; it is simultaneously kick and bass.
- **Glides/slides between notes** are the genre's melodic gesture.
- Distorted for phone audibility.
- Plays a simple riff, usually the chord roots with a tresillo rhythm:
```
808:    x - - x | - - x - | x - - - | - - x -
```

### Harmony and melody
- Minimal: a 2–4 chord minor loop, or a single-note ostinato.
- Melody instruments: bells, plucked strings, flutes, music box, choir, detuned
  pianos, Middle-Eastern-flavoured leads.
- Dark, sparse, often built from one 2-bar melodic loop with heavy reverb.
- Scales: natural minor, Phrygian, harmonic minor, Hungarian minor.

### Arrangement
```
0-7      Intro: melody loop alone.
8-15     Add drums, no 808.
16-31    Verse: full beat.
32-47    Hook/chorus: add layers, more energy.
48-63    Verse 2.
64-79    Hook.
80-87    Bridge / beat switch.
88-103   Final hook.
```
**The beat switch** — changing the entire instrumental halfway through — is a
modern convention worth using.

## Production notes

- **Leave the mid-range empty.** 300 Hz–3 kHz is the rapper's space. Melody
  instruments live above it or are heavily filtered.
- The 808 and the kick are the same element or must be carefully separated —
  if separate, use a short punchy kick and let the 808 carry all the sub.
- **Vocals sit in the beat**, not on top of it, in trap; in boom bap they sit
  clearly forward.
- Sidechain the 808 to the kick when they are separate elements.
- Sparse is correct. A trap beat with five elements is a full production.
- Ad-libs (panned wide, delayed, pitched) are half the vocal arrangement.

## Clichés (use knowingly)

The hi-hat triplet roll before the hook; the "beat drops out for the last line";
the pitched-up vocal ad-lib; the 808 slide down at the end of a phrase; the
dark bell melody; the producer tag at bar 1.

## Hazards

- A busy melody competing with the vocal.
- 808 with no mid-range harmonics — inaudible on phones.
- Straight, unvaried hi-hats.
- Loops with no variation over 4 minutes.
- Over-compressing the 808 (it kills the sub).
- Sampling without clearance for a commercial release.
